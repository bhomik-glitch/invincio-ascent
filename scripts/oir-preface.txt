PREFACE

From the Desk of the Founder & CEO

“An officer is not defined merely by what he knows, but by how he thinks, decides and acts when faced with a challenge.”

The journey towards becoming an officer in the Indian Armed Forces begins much before one enters the portals of a Services Selection Board. It begins with developing the ability to think clearly, reason logically, make timely decisions and remain composed under pressure.

The Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) Test is one of the first opportunities for an aspirant to demonstrate these abilities during the SSB selection process. While the questions may appear simple, the real challenge lies in achieving a fine balance between speed, accuracy and clarity of thought.

At Invincio Services LLP, our philosophy has always been clear — Mentorship, Not Coaching. We believe that genuine preparation is not about memorising answers or mastering shortcuts alone. It is about developing the mental agility, confidence and intellectual discipline that remain valuable far beyond the SSB.

This booklet, thoughtfully compiled by our team, is a step in that direction. It offers a structured collection of OIR practice tests, accompanied by answer sheets, to help aspirants assess their present abilities, identify areas for improvement and progressively enhance their performance.

I encourage every aspirant to approach these exercises with sincerity. Practise under timed conditions, evaluate your responses honestly, revisit your mistakes and, most importantly, understand the reasoning behind every correct answer.

Remember, the objective is not merely to clear a test. It is to cultivate the qualities of a thinking, responsible and decisive future leader.

Having spent years assessing and mentoring officer aspirants, I remain convinced that consistent effort, honest self-assessment and the willingness to learn are among the strongest foundations of success.

As you begin working through this booklet, remember that every question is an opportunity to improve, every mistake is an opportunity to learn, and every improvement brings you closer to your goal.

At Invincio, we do not merely prepare candidates for selection. We endeavour to prepare individuals for the responsibilities of leadership and life.

Wishing you clarity in thought, confidence in action and success in your journey.

Once Invincio, Always Invincible!

Jai Hind!

Lt Col Ankur Sabharwal
Military Veteran
Founder & CEO, Invincio Services LLP
Former GTO & Mentor of Assessors at DIPR
